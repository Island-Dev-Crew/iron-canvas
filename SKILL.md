### 0.1 — THE VOICE: What Iron Canvas Feels Like

> This is the voice of the method. It governs how we work and what we aim for; your client's
> DNA governs how their site looks. The studio's voice is never a client's palette
> (Anti-Pattern #3 Template Imposition).

**What we are.** *Raw Ideas. Refined Experiences.* Iron Canvas is an AI-powered design studio
that transforms digital presence into premium-grade experiences. **We don't vibe-code. We
engineer beauty.** Make any site the best version of ITSELF.

**The room we work in.** *"When someone lands on Iron Canvas, they should feel like they've
walked into a master blacksmith's studio — not a sterile SaaS homepage. The craft is visible.
The precision is tangible. Every element communicates: 'These people obsess over details you
didn't even know existed.'"*

**What the work should make people feel** — our own Feel Brief ([`FEEL.md`](FEEL.md), a worked
example of a finished one):
1. **Awe** — "This is a level above what I've seen"
2. **Trust** — "These people clearly know what they're doing"
3. **Aspiration** — "I want MY brand to feel like this"
4. **Warmth** — Premium without being cold or corporate
5. **Kinetic Energy — Everything feels alive, intentional, moving**

**What it must never feel like:** corporate SaaS template (Stripe/Linear clone) · cheap agency
with stock photos · AI-generated purple gradient slop · **overly minimalist (empty without
purpose)** · "we built this in a weekend" energy · **lifeless or mechanical** (v6 — the default
anti-feeling of every project, at every register).

**Feeling first.** *"Before a single pixel exists, we answer one question: how should your
audience feel when they arrive? This anchors every downstream decision — from color temperature
to font weight to motion speed. A feel brief is not a mood board. It's a lens."*

**Motion is the real touch.** *"High-end motion design is NOT about animating everything. It is
about intentional contrast between what moves and what stays still. The still elements make the
moving elements land harder. The moving elements make the still elements feel grounded."*
Motion without budget is noise; motion without feeling is decoration with a schedule.

**Breath.** *"Whitespace is not empty space. It is the breath between notes."* Twenty percent of
every viewport breathes.

**Craft.** *"Look. React. Describe. Fix. Look again. This loop runs until every pixel earns its
place. We review on mobile and desktop. We check spacing at 2am on a dim screen."*

**Stance.** In an existing site: *"You're a surgeon adding capability, not an architect
rebuilding from scratch. Every enhancement must feel like it was ALWAYS there."* In discovery:
*"The emotional target comes FROM the site, not from you. You are a detective, not an art
director."* In review: three critics at once — an Awwwards judge, a principal engineer, an
accessibility auditor. *"Be honest. Sycophancy here is expensive."*

**The Living Rules (v6 — the voice, made executable):**
1. **THE ALIVENESS FLOOR.** Every surface, R0 to R4, ships five things: **ARRIVAL** (one designed
   first moment, ≤ 1.5 s — never everything at once), **HEARTBEAT** (one perpetual, low-amplitude
   element on co-prime periods, so it never visibly loops), **HAND-FEEL** (every interactive
   element answers hover, press and focus), **BREATH** (air plus one ambient layer — grain felt,
   not seen; a light ladder; a mesh that breathes), and a **COMPOSED STILL** (reduced motion rests
   on the designed final frame — never blank). CD3 shapes their amplitude; nothing removes them.
   Missing one is **Anti-Pattern #18 LIFELESS**; VERIFY **Axis 7** proves them with recordings.
   *Restraint is not stillness.* → [`references/motion-language.md`](references/motion-language.md)
2. **WRITE THE SCORE.** Motion is authored, not sprinkled. Before a line of motion code, every
   section gets an emotion, a job and a role; the page gets one **motion sentence** (establish →
   accelerate → pause → reveal → recover → climax → resolve), exactly **one signature** and **one
   clock**. *The agent writes the score; the runtime performs it* (§23).
   → [`references/cinematic-score.md`](references/cinematic-score.md)
3. **ADJECTIVES DIRECT. EVIDENCE PROVES.** Sensory words — the lens, the reference vibe, each
   section's emotion — are required inputs. They are banned only as unproven completion claims.
4. **ONE CRAFT, EVERY ALTITUDE.** [`showcase/origin.html`](showcase/origin.html) — the first Iron
   Canvas page, restored — proves "alive, quiet, expensive" with zero libraries.
   [`showcase/living-canvas/`](showcase/living-canvas/) proves the same voice as a five-act WebGL
   film. Neither is a style to copy; both are the standard.

*"Every engagement applies the full Iron Canvas method. The deliverable format changes. The
standard never does."*

### 0.2 — CLAUDE DESIGN 3: The Treatment Doctrine (the conscience)

Iron Canvas's other systems answer *how much* (DIS), *how deep* (Depth Language), and
*how the brand feels* (Brand Personality Matrix). Claude Design 3 answers what sits
above them all: **what treatment does this task actually call for, and is it grounded
in something real?** Full spec — the conscience, made law — in
[references/claude-design-3.md](references/claude-design-3.md). The five laws:

1. **Calibrate the treatment, not whether to design.** The craft is constant; the
   register is chosen. One craft across a high-dynamic-range span:

   | | Register | The task | "Full craft" here | DIS ceiling (CAP — max, not a floor) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | **R0** | Utilitarian | tool / admin / docs / report | information design; restraint IS the deliverable | 0.0–0.2 |
   | **R1** | Functional | SaaS / marketing that must convert | a thesis hero + one memorable moment | 0.2–0.5 |
   | **R2** | Editorial | a page they keep or share | an opinionated POV, type with character | 0.4–0.7 |
   | **R3** | Maximalist | a flagship / Awwwards run | layered depth, grid-breaking, signature technique | 0.6–0.9 |
   | **R4** | Immersive | a Type-A world | inhabited depth, camera rail, Agent F + §22 gate | 0.8–1.0 |

   Register comes from the **task's ambition**, set at ORIENT (`treatment_register` in
   `orient-decision.json`); it caps the DIS ceiling. It is orthogonal to the BPM
   (the brand's voice). **When unsure, drop a register — never below the aliveness floor
   (§0.1). Restraint is not stillness.** An over-produced R3 page on an R0 task is design
   malpractice; a crafted R0 dashboard that breathes is a win; a dead one is Anti-Pattern #18.
2. **Ground it in the subject (the soul).** Distinctive design comes from the subject's
   own world — materials, vocabulary, the real job. Real content, never lorem. Copy is
   design material. Build for the one unforgettable moment. Structure encodes truth
   (numbered markers only for real sequences). A stranger must be able to name the
   subject from the design alone.
3. **The non-negotiable fundamentals** (every register): typography as personality
   (deliberate pairing, no default stacks); choose neutrals, don't default; design both
   themes token-level; layout does the spacing; spend boldness in one place; when it's a
   UI, the craft is information design. **A design plan before code** (★v6, the Claude design
   method): 4–6 named colours with roles, 1–2 type families, a deliberate scale — reviewed for
   genericness *before* the first component is built.
4. **The anti-slop canon** (fails VERIFY on sight unless the DNA pins it): cream+serif+
   terracotta "editorial"; a lone acid pop as the whole idea; Inter/Space Grotesk as the
   "safe" face; emoji section markers; everything centered; `rounded-lg` everywhere;
   purple→blue gradient hero; uniform radius/shadow with no hierarchy; decorative numbered
   markers; library defaults shipped as finished design. Full veto list in anti-patterns.
   **★v6 — the registry grows every run.** A general "avoid the AI look" only swaps one default
   for another; named negatives work. Current model defaults to question on sight (each needs a
   reason from the DNA — with one they are choices, without one they are defaults): cream or
   off-white grounds, an italic accent word in every headline, "01 / 02 / 03" section labels,
   monospace eyebrow labels, pill buttons, "→" appended to every link. After the first render,
   the Art Director names what it saw that reads as default and appends it to the project's
   veto list (PRD §11).
5. **CD3 composes, never duplicates.** ORIENT picks the register → STUDY/FEEL harvest the
   soul → FORGE reads the DIS *within* the register → VERIFY adds **Axis 6 (Treatment &
   Soul)** and **Axis 7 (Aliveness)**. No authority without evidence — **and no evidence
   without intent**: the register is on the record, the anti-slop pass is captured — never
   asserted.

### ★v5.2 — THE GAUNTLET BUILD METHOD (generative phases, R2–R4)

At registers **R2–R4**, the build method for the **generative** phases — **FORGE(4)**,
**IMMERSE(4.5)**, **GENERATE(5)** — is the **gauntlet loop**, not one-shot-and-refine: fan out
N candidates (**Agent-A/C** for FORGE directions — token+shell grammar, hero, key sections;
**Agent F** for worlds; **Agent-B/D** for scenes/scroll/§19 systems), each `worktree-fleet`-
isolated, shadow each with a **blind `cross-family-review` critic** scoring against the
falsifiable bar (pack §5 VERIFY + the 7-axis audit read at the register + a North Star
reference-peg screenshot diff), loop until one clears, ship the winner. **OFF at R0/R1** — at R1
a single signature moment may loop; looping a utilitarian build is **Anti-Pattern #17**.
**COMPOSE(6) is excluded** — it assembles the winners, it does not gauntlet. Never start cold
(runs only after Phases 0–3.9 produce the Design PRD + North Star). Graduation: **gauntlet for
wow, Phase-7 VERIFY for proof.** Full protocol: [`references/gauntlet-loop.md`](references/gauntlet-loop.md).
★v6: the critic gains a fourth lens — a creative director asking *"Does the motion serve the
story, or is it noise?"*, *"What would make someone say 'show me that again'?"* and *"Which section
feels leftover?"* — and scores Axis 7 (Aliveness) from the recordings.

#### §7b — CHOREOGRAPHY ★v6 (the Motion Architecture, restored inline)

**The load choreography** — *"Every site gets ONE load choreography — make it memorable."*
```
Phase 0 (0–200 ms)      ground, atmosphere, canvas init — the heartbeat is already turning
Phase 1 (200–600 ms)    primary visual / 3D element
Phase 2 (400–800 ms)    headline — line, word or character stagger
Phase 3 (600–1000 ms)   supporting text
Phase 4 (800–1200 ms)   CTA
Phase 5 (1000–1400 ms)  navigation + secondary UI
Total ≤ 1500 ms · steps 50–150 ms · hero on the house personality, support gentler · NEVER all at once
```

**The personalities** (one vocabulary — GSAP, CSS and the runtime share it):
```
silk     expo.out     1.4 s  stagger .035  a heavy object on a damper — lands, then exhales (the original curve)
tide     sine.inOut   2.4 s  stagger .08   water arriving; contemplative
gravity  power4.out   1.1 s  stagger .05   weight, authority, arrival
spark    back.out(1.7) 0.6 s stagger .025  a small overshoot of joy — also the tick (≤ 0.25 s) everywhere
snap     power3.inOut 0.5 s  stagger .02   precision; a decisive cut
bloom    expo.inOut   1.8 s  stagger .06   light opening — the signature reveal, once
drift    none (scrub)                     the camera travelling; depth by velocity
ONE house personality + at most ONE accent (the signature) · quiet acts may rest on tide ·
scroll-owned motion is drift · ≤ 3 duration families (hands · entrances · ambience)
```

**The three tempos:** hands 0.15–0.5 s · entrances 0.8–1.4 s with 100–150 ms steps and 28–48 px
travel · ambience 4–36 s on **co-prime periods** (the original orb: rings at 18 / 28 / 12 s realign
only every 252 s — it never visibly loops).

**Transitions by feeling:**
```
fade-through-black → Cinematic     curtain (Iron Curtain) → Theatrical     shared-element morph → Seamless
colour flood → Brand-forward        WebGL distortion → Psychedelic (R3+)    zoom in/out → Magazine
```

**The scene map** — every section, no blanks (the PRD lint rejects an empty Emotion):
```
[SECTION / act id]  Stage: [motion-sentence beat]   Job: [orient / explain_system / … / commit]
  Load: …   Scroll: …   Cursor: …   Exit: …   Duration: …   Technique / verbs: …
  Emotion: [what the visitor should feel here — in → out]   Role: [signature / support / stillness]
  Still: [what the settled frame means under reduced motion]
```

**React / Next.js:** never mix GSAP and Framer Motion in one tree · no `useState` for anything that
updates at 60 fps · perpetual motion lives in its own memoized client component · wrap GSAP in
`gsap.context(() => { … }, ref)` and `return () => ctx.revert()` · IntersectionObserver or
`whileInView`, never `window.addEventListener('scroll')`. Full rules:
[`references/motion-budget.md`](references/motion-budget.md) "React / Next.js Motion Rules".

        gsap.to(this.ring, { scale: 2.5, duration: 0.3 }));
      el.addEventListener('mousemove', (e) => {
        const r = el.getBoundingClientRect();
        gsap.to(el, {
          x: (e.clientX - r.left - r.width/2) * 0.3,
          y: (e.clientY - r.top  - r.height/2) * 0.3,
          duration: 0.3
        });
      });
      el.addEventListener('mouseleave', () => {
        gsap.to(el, { x: 0, y: 0, duration: 0.5, ease: 'elastic.out(1,0.3)' });
        gsap.to(this.ring, { scale: 1, duration: 0.3 });
      });
    });
    this.render();
  }

    gsap.set(this.dot,  { x: this.mouse.x, y: this.mouse.y });

```javascript
// Full Canvas scroll engine — SCROLL-TIED motion ALWAYS uses Canvas, NEVER <video>
// WHY: Canvas = bidirectional scrub + per-frame content sync + mobile reliable
//      video = unidirectional + no scroll scrub + mobile autoplay restrictions
// (A LOOPING background is a muted <video> — that is fine. Scroll-tied is never video.
//  Mobile fallback for heavy sequences: fewer frames (frames-mobile/) or a static poster.)

const TOTAL_FRAMES = 60;
const frames = [];
let loadedCount = 0;

async function preloadFrames() {
  return new Promise((resolve) => {
    for (let i = 1; i <= TOTAL_FRAMES; i++) {
      const img = new Image();
      img.src = `/frames/frame_${String(i).padStart(4,'0')}.webp`;
      img.onload = () => {
        loadedCount++;
        document.querySelector('.loader-progress').style.width =
          `${(loadedCount/TOTAL_FRAMES)*100}%`;
        if (loadedCount === TOTAL_FRAMES) resolve();
      };
      frames.push(img);
    }
  });
}

const canvas = document.getElementById('scroll-canvas');
const ctx = canvas.getContext('2d');

function drawFrame(index) {
  const img = frames[Math.min(index, TOTAL_FRAMES-1)];
  if (!img) return;
  const scale = Math.max(canvas.width/img.naturalWidth, canvas.height/img.naturalHeight);
  const x = (canvas.width - img.naturalWidth*scale) / 2;
  const y = (canvas.height - img.naturalHeight*scale) / 2;
  ctx.clearRect(0, 0, canvas.width, canvas.height);
  ctx.drawImage(img, x, y, img.naturalWidth*scale, img.naturalHeight*scale);
}

preloadFrames().then(() => {
  document.querySelector('#frame-loader').style.display = 'none';
  drawFrame(0);

  gsap.to({ frame: 0 }, {
    frame: TOTAL_FRAMES - 1, snap: 'frame', ease: 'none',
    scrollTrigger: {
      trigger: '#scroll-section',
      start: 'top top', end: 'bottom bottom',
      scrub: 0.5, pin: canvas
    },
    onUpdate: function() {
      drawFrame(Math.round(this.targets()[0].frame));
    }
  });

  // Content overlays synced per-frame to scroll (from Design PRD overlay timing)
  gsap.fromTo('.overlay-1', { opacity: 0, y: 40 }, {
    opacity: 1, y: 0, scrollTrigger: {
      trigger: '#scroll-section', start: '10% top', end: '20% top', scrub: true
    }
  });
});
```

### 9. One-Shot Prompting ★v4
**What it looks like:** Opening a coding editor and typing "build me a dashboard" then prompting linearly — fixing errors, fighting linters, rewriting components, wondering why the UI keeps drifting.
**Why it happens:** Treating AI coding agents as junior developers who can guess your stack, layout logic, and standards.
**How to prevent:** Iron Canvas's entire pipeline. Discovery Interview (Phase 0) injects business logic. Design PRD (Phase 3.9) is the immutable source of truth. The skill repo IS persistent context that eliminates guessing.

### 10. Skipping Asset Pipeline ★v4
**What it looks like:** Dropping raw video files directly into the page as scroll-driven animation. Files are too heavy, can't control playback with scroll, performance collapses.
**Why it happens:** Not understanding the difference between looping background video and scroll-tied frame sequences.
**How to prevent:** EVERY asset gets classified: scroll-tied → frames at 15fps, looping background → autoplay video, static → optimized image. Never hand raw video to a scroll engine. Classify → optimize → THEN integrate.

### 11. Context Drift ★v4
**What it looks like:** AI agents guess your tech stack, design system, and business logic because you never defined the rules. Each prompt gets further from the original intent. UI drifts over time.
**Why it happens:** No persistent context injection. One-shot prompting without a source of truth.
**How to prevent:** The skill repo is the "universal repository." Discovery Interview captures intent. Design PRD locks in decisions. All agents reference the same tokens and the same PRD. Context drift is structurally impossible when the pipeline is followed.

Enforced via §0 Law 4, the anti-patterns file, and Agent-E QA — not repeated here to avoid a divergent copy.

### 18. LIFELESS ★v6
**What it looks like:** A page — or a dashboard, or a tool — that is correct and dead. Nothing
arrives; it is simply there. Nothing breathes at rest. Buttons change colour and nothing else.
Sections sit on flat grounds with no air and no light. Reduced motion shows a blank. The
"overly minimalist (empty without purpose)" and "we built this in a weekend" energy FEEL.md
warned about on day one.
**Why it happens:** Restraint misread as stillness. Register caps, SOLO ceilings and "when
unsure, drop a register" applied to the floor instead of the ceiling. Evidence gates that measure
discipline (no jank, no errors) but never life.
**How to prevent:** The aliveness floor (§0.1 Living Rule 1) at every register — arrival,
heartbeat, hand-feel, breath, composed still — proven by VERIFY Axis 7. It is the counterweight
to #17: under-producing fails as surely as over-producing.

### 19. TWO CLOCKS ★v6
**What it looks like:** Two things own the scroll in one viewport — a pinned GSAP section and a
WebGL camera reading `window.scrollY`, or a parallax library fighting Lenis. Jumps stutter,
`seek` lands in the wrong place, reduced motion still scrubs, the film and the page drift apart.
**Why it happens:** Motion coordinated through prose, each agent wiring its own listener.
**How to prevent:** One clock. Only the score's `scrub` acts own scroll; Lenis only smooths native
scroll; the WebGL scene and every DOM chapter subscribe to the runtime's progress; programmatic
jumps route through `lenis.scrollTo`. `scripts/ic-preflight.mjs` rejects a score with a second owner.

**Craft Wisdom (v4 — from Samir's Applied AI Engineering):**

**Feature Discovery Post-Build:**
After the initial build is stable, ask the agent: "What other features can we add
to make this more appealing?" This often surfaces sound design, custom cursor
refinements, and micro-interactions that weren't in the original scope.

**Post-Build Micro-Interaction Pass:**
Sound design, custom cursors, and micro-interactions are added AFTER the main
build, not during. Agent-C runs two sweeps: Core Pass → Polish Pass.

## SECTION 16: § TASTE DOCTRINE
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

**1. RESTRAINT IS A SUPERPOWER**
3 animations that wow > 30 that tire.

**2. TYPOGRAPHY IS THE DESIGN**
Fix type before adding any effect. If it can't survive as text-only, the problem is the type.
If the type isn't right, no animation will save it.

**3. COLOR TELLS THE EMOTIONAL STORY**
Palette communicates before the first word is read. Design it to feel right in 3 seconds.

**4. ANIMATION REVEALS QUALITY, DOESN'T CREATE IT**
Get the static version right. THEN animate. A bad design animated is a bad design moving.

**5. THE ONE UNFORGETTABLE THING**
Design for the single moment people screenshot. Everything else is context leading to it.

**6. COPY TECHNIQUE, NOT STYLE**
Acceptable: same GSAP technique as an Awwwards winner.
Not acceptable: copying their visual language.

**7. THE 15-SECOND SCROLL**
Users scroll your site in 15 seconds on first pass. Best moments must be visible in that window.

**8. MOBILE IS NOT A DOWNGRADE**
Different canvas, different physics, different constraints.
Preserve ONE signature animation. Never zero.

**9. NOTHING SHIPS LIFELESS ★v6**
Restraint decides how much moves — never whether anything breathes. Arrival, heartbeat,
hand-feel, breath, a composed still: at every register, on every surface.

**10. ONE CLOCK, ONE SIGNATURE ★v6**
Write the score before the code. One motion sentence, one unforgettable moment, one thing that
owns the scroll. The quiet chapter is what makes the climax land.

## SECTION 17: § DESIGN INTENSITY SCALE (DIS) ★v4.2
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
### *One design language. The Treatment Register caps the whole scale (task ambition); Mode sets the ceiling (horsepower); Brand DNA sets the floor; ★v6 the aliveness floor sits under everything and no cap removes it.*

Iron Canvas has a design-language CEILING — the most visually impressive output the
system can produce. Every project inherits from this ceiling at a scaled intensity:

```
DESIGN INTENSITY = f(Treatment Register cap, Execution Mode ceiling, Brand Personality Matrix scores)
  Treatment Register (CD3 §0) — the OUTERMOST cap, by TASK AMBITION (R0–R4); applied last
  Mode sets the ceiling (max available intensity, by team horsepower)
  BPM scores set the FLOOR + OVERRIDES (DNA-driven activation)
```

The mode never forces a feature ON — it only caps how many can activate.
The BPM scores never force a feature OFF — they can UNLOCK systems even in lower
modes when the DNA demands it — **except** the Treatment Register cap (CD3 §0), the
outermost clamp, which bounds even a BPM unlock down to the task's register (an R0
utilitarian task clamps a bold=9 unlock to ≤ 0.2). **The language scales DOWN as
carefully as it scales UP.**

### The 9 design systems (each dialed 0.0–1.0)

| 1 | Gradient Mesh Atmosphere | A | oklch orbs, blur diffusion, ambient depth |
| 2 | Glass Morphism Depth | A+C | backdrop-filter, tinted borders, inner glow |
| 3 | Kinetic Typography | C | char stagger, scramble, display scale |
| 4 | Particle Field | B | ambient particles, density, depth |
| 5 | Border Luminance | C | glowing edges, pulse, hover amplification |
| 6 | Scroll-Tied Sequences | B+D | canvas frames bound to ScrollTrigger |
| 7 | Magnetic Cursor | C | custom cursor, attraction fields, WebGL |
| 8 | Micro-Interaction Polish | C+E | sound, toasts, loading, delight |
| 9 | Depth Language ★v5.0 | A+B+C | z-axis grammar — composed / staged / inhabited (§20) |

**★v6 — what each dial FEELS like** (a number without a feeling is how the doctrine went
mechanical; every dial gets its felt description back):

| # | Low (0.1–0.3) | Mid (0.4–0.6) | High (0.8–1.0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Mesh atmosphere | a ground that is not quite flat — light pooling in one corner | a mesh that breathes on 20–40 s loops | weather — colour moving like light through smoke |
| 2 Glass depth | one frosted surface where it earns it | glass strata — layers you can almost touch | a glass world; every surface refracts the one below |

**Sound (inside System 8) ★v6** — invited, never imposed: off by default behind a visible toggle;

### Mode ceilings

```
MISSION  → 9/9 systems, intensity ceiling 1.0  (all active by default)
SWARM    → 7/9 systems, intensity ceiling 0.7  (particles + cursor OFF unless BPM overrides)
SOLO     → 4/9 systems, intensity ceiling 0.4  (mesh-subtle + type + polish + composed depth)
Depth (System 9) is never OFF — every project carries ≥ 0.1 composed depth. The mode ceiling
caps its ALTITUDE: SOLO ≤ 0.35 (Tier I) · SWARM ≤ 0.7 (Tier II) · MISSION ≤ 1.0 (Tier III).
★v6 The ALIVENESS FLOOR (§0.1) is never OFF either. Mode ceilings, register caps and BPM
suppression shape its AMPLITUDE — a 600 ms arrival and one breathing dot at SOLO/R0 — and never
remove it. A dial at 0.0 means "this system is off", not "this page is dead".
```

### BPM override rule

```
Any BPM axis ≥ 8 that maps to a disabled system → UNLOCK it (capped at mode ceiling).
Any suppressing axis (e.g. classic ≥ 8, minimal ≥ 8, serious ≥ 9) → CAP that system DOWN,
  even in MISSION mode.
```

Examples:
- SOLO + bold=9, avant_garde=8 → cursor + particles UNLOCK at 0.4 (DNA demands it, capped by SOLO)
- MISSION + minimal=9, serious=9 → particles 0.2, cursor 0.3, mesh 0.3 (restrained even at max mode)

extracted colors (Phase 1) + (2) the BPM scores. Warm DNA → warm palette; cool/technical DNA →

brand whose only cue was a gold button — never a house violet.

```
organic ≥ 6 → warm earth hues (oklch 40–95)   |  digital ≥ 6 + organic ≤ 3 → cool (220–280)
refined ≥ 7 → low-chroma base + 1 bright accent | bold ≥ 7 → dark dramatic base + saturated accent
minimal ≥ 7 → 2–3 colors                        | contemplative ≥ 6 → deeper/calmer luminance
SELF-CHECK: palette must be unmistakably the brand's — generic violet/blue = Anti-Pattern #5, re-derive.
```

## SECTION 18: § CODE-DRIVEN ASSETS ★v4.2
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
### *The fourth asset class. Deterministic. No AI cost. Infinite resolution.*

v4 classified assets as scroll-tied / looping / static — all AI-generated.
**v4.2 adds CODE-DRIVEN:** assets defined in code, rendered deterministically.

```
DECISION RULE:
  Photographic / organic / textural  → AI engine (Nano Banana, Whisk, Flow, Leonardo)
  Geometric / data / logo / exact UI  → CODE-DRIVEN (SVG, Remotion, Hyperframes, Algo Art)

### Asset Classification Taxonomy — v4.2 (4 classes)

```
SCROLL-TIED       → 15fps JPEG frames → Canvas + ScrollTrigger
LOOPING BG        → MP4 autoplay+loop+muted → behind content
STATIC            → WebP/AVIF optimized → lazy below fold
CODE-DRIVEN ★v4.2 → SVG inline / Remotion render / Hyperframe def / Algo Art
                    → may stay LIVE (SVG/canvas) OR render into one of the above
```

CODE-DRIVEN is both a source class and resolves INTO others:
Hyperframe → SCROLL-TIED frames · Remotion OG card → LOOPING BG · animated SVG → stays live.
**When an asset can stay live (SVG), it should** — zero payload, infinite resolution, editable.

### Three code-driven capabilities

**1. SVG GRAPHICS** (Agent-C inline + Agent-D standalone)
Self-drawing logos (stroke-dashoffset), path morphing (MorphSVG/flubber/SMIL), generative
patterns, animated data viz, icon systems, SVG filters (feTurbulence/feDisplacementMap).
Intensity 0.1→1.0 scales static icons → full DrawSVG choreography + liquid filters.

**2. REMOTION** (Agent-D author + Agent-B integrate)
React → real video, frame-by-frame deterministic via `useCurrentFrame()`.
Data-driven motion graphics, animated OG video, designed scroll sequences (pixel-exact,
no drift). Replaces v4 Keyframe Interpolation for DESIGNED motion:
```
Organic transition (smoke, liquid) → Google Flow (v4 AI interpolation)
Designed/data/typographic motion   → Remotion (v4.2 code interpolation)
```
Render → frames at 15fps → ScrollTrigger (same Phase 6 frame protocol). OG video extends
the mandatory OG image: render og-video.mp4 + still og-image.jpg fallback.

**3. HYPERFRAMES** (Agent-B define + Agent-D render)
Code-defined high-fidelity frame sequences: `frame(i) = render(scene, i/total)`.
The deterministic evolution of Keyframe Interpolation — no drift, re-render at any res.
```
Method A — SVG Hyperframes (lightest; often stays live, no extraction)
Method B — Remotion Hyperframes (richest; typographic/data/layered)
Method C — Canvas/WebGL Hyperframes (3D, particles, product 360°)
```
Hyperframe gate: scroll sequence needed → photographic? AI path. Geometric/data? Hyperframe.

**★v6 — HyperFrames is now a real renderer.** HeyGen's HyperFrames (Apache-2.0) renders plain
HTML + GSAP / three.js / CSS into MP4, MOV with alpha, WebM with alpha or PNG sequences — with a
determinism contract (seeked timelines, no wall clock, seeded randomness) and `lint` / `check` /
`snapshot` commands. It is the default film renderer for PREMIERE (§11) and for designed motion in
this section; the v4.2 "Hyperframe" method (code-defined frame sequences) is what it performs.
Remotion stays an adapter (company licence above 3 employees).

→ FULL SPEC: references/code-driven-assets.md

### *Premium is a score, not a vibe claim. ★v6: Adjectives direct. Evidence proves.*

IMMERSIVE is the evidence-gated execution lane for Depth Language work. It adds **Agent F
(Immersion)** and **Phase 4.5 IMMERSE** between FORGE and GENERATE.

```

  Tier II STAGED  (0.4–0.7):  Agent F builds the one staged scene (§20 rules of the stage)
  Tier III INHABITED (0.75–1.0): Agent F is the WORLD AGENT — camera rail, world material,

### Agent F — Immersion Specialist

harness. Never restructures pages (A/C), never touches unrelated scroll work (B).
Full prompt: agent-prompts/agent-f-immersion.md · Stack ref: references/webgl-immersion.md

### The gate (zero tolerance — any auto-fail blocks Phase 5)
```
templates/immersion-scorecard.md average ≥ 4.0 AND zero automatic failures:
  ✗ console/page errors          ✗ mobile horizontal overflow
  ✗ reduced-motion still animating  ✗ no static fallback / no kill switch
  ✗ no evidence screenshots (desktop + mobile + reduced-motion)
  ✗ ★v6 a mounted-but-black canvas (frames drawn = 0 or lit-pixel ratio below threshold)
  ✗ ★v6 nothing alive at rest (Axis 7) · two scroll owners (Anti-Pattern #19)
Harness: templates/playwright.config.ts + templates/smoke.spec.ts
         ★v6 templates/scroll-capture.spec.ts — steps the scroll, dwells, screenshots every act

```
The gate IS the §20 fallback-ladder rule (Hard Rule 2), proven in a browser: Tier III must
demonstrate its Tier II degradation, Tier II its Tier I static frame. Claimed = tested.

### Doctrine (v4.3, upheld — ★v6 reframed)
One unforgettable immersive moment beats ten scattered effects. WebGL is progressive
enhancement, never the only usable experience. **Adjectives direct. Evidence proves.** v4.3's
"evidence beats adjectives" was right about completion claims and wrong as a writing style: the
sensory words — the lens, the reference vibe, each act's emotion — are required *inputs* to FEEL
and the score. They are banned only as unproven *claims*. No authority without evidence, and no
evidence without intent.

## SECTION 23: § THE SCORE — THE SCORE RUNTIME ★v6
## ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
### *The agent writes the score. The runtime performs it.*

The original doctrine was alive but fragile: every run hand-wrote its own animation code, and
every run could break it. v6 moves the fragile part into one tested engine
([`runtime/canvas-score.js`](runtime/canvas-score.js)). Agents author **intent** — acts, shots,
verbs, personalities — and the runtime performs it with the reduced-motion, static and
kill-switch paths built in. The score is also the **motion contract** every agent and the critic
read ([`references/cinematic-score.md`](references/cinematic-score.md)).

```
THE SCORE     { title, logline, personality, tempo, seed, breath, signature, acts[] }
AN ACT        one section: id · el · stage (motion-sentence beat) · job · role (signature | support |
              stillness) · intent · emotion {in, out} · trigger (load | enter | scrub) · personality ·
              pin / length / scrub / hold · reduced (what the still frame means) · shots[]
A SHOT        a verb on the act's timeline: at · target · verb · split · personality · duration ·
              stagger · note (the director's note) — or a hook: call · args (e.g. the WebGL peak)
17 VERBS      enter: fade · rise · unveil · bloom · focus · tilt · wipe · iris
              exit: sink · lift · defocus · dolly   scrub: drift   special: draw · count · scramble · sweep
7 PERSONALITIES silk · tide · gravity · spark · snap · bloom · drift  (references/motion-language.md)
MODES         full · reduced (prefers-reduced-motion / ?motion=reduced: opacity only, no pins, no
              breath, native scroll) · static (?motion=off / GSAP absent: final states only)
CONTROLLER    show.ready · show.on('progress'|'page'|'enter'|'leave') · show.seek(act, p) ·
              show.kill() (the kill switch) · window.__icScore (evidence) · ?score=debug
```

**Wiring (vanilla):** mark `ic-js` on `<html>` before first paint · hide `[data-ic]` entrance
targets with a 3 s CSS failsafe so nothing can stay hidden · load GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis ·
`performScore(score, { hooks })`. **React / Next:** call it from one client component and `kill()`
on unmount, or port the verbs with the same names. **WebGL:** subscribe to `progress`; never read
scroll. **Preflight:** `node scripts/ic-preflight.mjs` enforces one signature, one clock, known
verbs, a feeling and a still meaning on every act. Live bench: [`runtime/verbs.html`](runtime/verbs.html).

LEDGER        candidates → blind critic → select (a written reason) → verify (re-hash) → encode →
