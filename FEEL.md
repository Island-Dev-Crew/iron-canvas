# FEEL.md — Iron Canvas's own Feel Brief

> **What this file is.** The Iron Canvas studio's own feel brief — the first one ever written for
> the method (March 2026), kept as a worked example of a finished one. It describes how *our*
> surfaces should feel. It is never a palette or a style for a client: a client's DNA governs how
> their site looks (Anti-Pattern #3 Template Imposition). SKILL.md §0.1 quotes it; the restored
> first page, [`showcase/origin.html`](showcase/origin.html), is what it looks like built.

## The lens
*How should the audience feel when they arrive?* Like they have stepped into a working forge after
dark — warm light on metal, patient hands, and the sense that every object in the room was made on
purpose.

## Emotional Target
When someone lands on Iron Canvas, they should feel like they've walked into a master blacksmith's studio — not a sterile SaaS homepage. The craft is visible. The precision is tangible. Every element communicates: "These people obsess over details you didn't even know existed."

**First 3 seconds:** awe, held quietly — a mechanism already turning before a word appears.
**After:** aspiration — "I want MY brand to feel like this."

## Target User
Creative directors, startup founders, and brand owners who've seen enough AI-generated websites to know the difference — and want something that makes their competitors wonder who built it.

## Emotions to Evoke
1. **Awe** — "This is a level above what I've seen"
2. **Trust** — "These people clearly know what they're doing"
3. **Aspiration** — "I want MY brand to feel like this"
4. **Warmth** — Premium without being cold or corporate
5. **Kinetic Energy** — Everything feels alive, intentional, moving

## Must NOT Feel Like
1. Corporate SaaS template (Stripe/Linear clone)
2. Cheap agency with stock photos
3. AI-generated purple gradient slop
4. Overly minimalist (empty without purpose)
5. "We built this in a weekend" energy
6. Lifeless or mechanical — correct and dead *(v6: the default anti-feeling of every project)*

## Reference Vibe
Walking into a blacksmith's studio at night — one forge glowing, iron cooling on the anvil, the
smell of heat, and a craftsman who does not hurry.

## Visual Language Keywords
Forged, cinematic, editorial, warm metallics, haptic depth, tinted darkness, gold-on-obsidian, kinetic precision, analog warmth in digital space

**Feel line:** forged · patient · warm · iron · slow

## Kinetic Signature *(v6)*
The motion vocabulary is [`references/motion-language.md`](references/motion-language.md); these
are the studio's choices from it.

| | Iron Canvas's own surfaces |
|---|---|
| **Personality** | `silk` — expo-out, the calm exhale: every piece lands at once, then settles for most of a second |
| **Accent** | `bloom`, once — light opening on the promise |
| **Arrival** | the heartbeat is already turning at first paint; the words rise in a 150 ms cascade and are still by 1.7 s |
| **Heartbeat** | the iron orb — three hairline rings at 18 s / 28 s / 12 s (they realign only every 252 s), four glowing beads, a core that breathes 12 px every 6 s |
| **Hand-feel** | a gold hairline draws across a row on hover; buttons lift 2 px on the tick and bloom; arrows lean 4 px; press is a physical push |
| **Breath** | 10rem of dark air between sections; a still grain film at 3.2 % over everything; one gold hue at about sixteen opacities |
| **Signature moment** | the mechanism turning beside the manifesto — the method, visibly at work |
| **Still path** | the orb at rest at a composed angle, beads lit, every word in place |

## Selected Archetypes
- **Vibe:** Ethereal Glass (deep OLED black, radial mesh gradients, vantablack surfaces) BLENDED with Editorial Luxury (warm tones via gold accent, film grain, physical feel)
- **Layout:** Asymmetrical Bento (masonry-like CSS Grid with varying card sizes) + Editorial Split (massive typography left, interactive elements right)
